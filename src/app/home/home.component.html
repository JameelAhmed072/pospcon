Welcome To The 11th POSPCON 2026

It is our great pleasure to invite you to the 10th Paediatric Orthopaedic Conference – POSPCON 2025. This conference will be held in the historical city Lahore on 20 – 22 February 2025 at The Children’s Hospital / University of Child Health Sciences, Lahore. Paediatric Orthopaedic Society of Pakistan has completed a decade of its Annual Meeting and moving to double digit, a milestone hence this will be an extravagant academic event. POSPCON 2025 has attracted global renowned leader of Paediatric Orthopaedics from Australia, USA, Singapore, India, Qatar, UAE and Germany along with eminent national faculty.

This conference is designed for you to interact with the expert to share and enhance your knowledge. There will be sessions on Hip, Foot, Neuromuscular, Trauma, Infection Deformity correction & interactive case discussion. Pre conference on Paediatric Foot, Paediatric Trauma and Deformity Correction are also organised. We would like to extend our sincere invitation to all of you to join us on 21 – 22 February at Lahore. We are sure this scientific extravaganza will advance your Paediatric Orthopaedic knowledge and expertise. Looking forward to welcoming you at POSPCON 2025.
