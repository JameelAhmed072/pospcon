Welcome to POSPCON Balochistan Chapter 2026

Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC), Karachi, Pakistan

11th Paediatric Orthopaedic Conference – POSPCON 2026, taking place in the scenic and historic city of Quetta from April 2nd to 4th, 2026. As the Paediatric Orthopaedic Society of Pakistan completed decade of academic excellence, New milestone of society starting 2nd decade to conducting 1st conference in Baluchistan capital Quettta, city of hospitality and rich culture. This landmark conference marks our transition into a new era of learning, collaboration, and innovation. POSPCON 2026 will bring together world-renowned paediatric orthopaedic surgery experts from The United States, India, the UAE, Germany, Brazil, China , Turkey and national faculty across Pakistan. This diverse and distinguished faculty ensures an enriching academic experience for all participants. We are honoured to welcome the Republic of Turkey as our Guest Nation, celebrating our shared commitment to advancing paediatric orthopaedic care and education.

The scientific program will include focused sessions on:Paediatric Hip Conditions, Foot and Ankle Disorders, Neuromuscular Challenges, Trauma Management, Musculoskeletal Infections, Deformity Correction, Case-Based Discussions We are also hosting pre-conference coruse and workshops on: Paediatric Foot Disorders, Trauma, Hip surgeries and cerebral palsy Whether you are a practising surgeon, a trainee, or a researcher, POSPCON 2026 is designed to deepen your knowledge and refine your skills through interactive sessions and hands-on learning. We warmly invite you to join us in Quetta and be part of this milestone academic celebration. Looking forward to welcoming you at POSPCON 2026.
